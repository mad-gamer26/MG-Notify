    Sound

    While the site is open and in view, new notifications are announced to screen readers and play this sound instead of showing a pop-up. When the site is closed, your system's notification sound plays instead: browsers don't allow custom sounds there.

    Sound when a notification arrives while this site is open

      Sounds must be 30 seconds or less, in a format this browser plays (usually WAV, MP3, M4A or OGG). They're kept in this browser only.

      Browser notifications arrive even when this page is closed, as long as the browser is running. On iPhone and iPad, add this site to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and turn them on from there. More about browser notifications.
