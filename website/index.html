Push notifications from anything

MG Notify sends a notification to your phone, tablet or browser whenever a script, program, server or email asks it to. All it takes is one web address.

Create a free account Open your dashboard Read the documentation

How it works

  1. Create an account here or in the app, and verify your email address.
  2. Sign in on your devices. Use the MG Notify app on iPhone or Android, or turn on notifications in this browser.
  3. Create an API key in your dashboard or the app.
  4. Send. Open a link like https://notify.mad-gamer.com/api/notify?key=YOUR_KEY&msg=Hello from anything that can make a web request, or email YOUR_KEY@notify.mad-gamer.com. Every device you're signed in on gets it.

What you can do

Notify from anywhere

Shell scripts, cron jobs, home automation, game servers, build pipelines and spreadsheets: if it can fetch a web address, it can notify you.

Email to notification

Send or forward an email to your key's address and it arrives as a notification, with the full email one tap away.

All your devices

iPhone, Android and any modern browser. Sign in to several accounts on one device, and choose your own notification sound.

Built for everyone

The apps and this site are designed to work with screen readers, keyboards, braille displays and large text from the start.

Get MG Notify

  • iPhone and iPad: search for MG Notify in the App Store.
  • Android: download MG Notify for Android (version 2.0, 10 MB; Android 8 or later). How to install it.
  • Any browser: sign in, then turn on browser notifications in your dashboard.
